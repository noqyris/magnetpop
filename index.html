Privacy Policy

Magnet Pop: Satisfying Puzzle · Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Magnet Pop ("the app", "we", "us"), published by Noqyris, handles information when you use the app. We built Magnet Pop to be a calm, offline puzzle, and we keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the app and show ads.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, or other information that directly identifies you. Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google and its partners may collect the following categories of data from your device:

Data typePurposeUsed to track you
Device identifiers (including the advertising identifier / IDFA)Third-party advertisingYes*
Advertising data (e.g. which ads were shown)Third-party advertisingYes*
Product interaction (e.g. ad taps, app usage events)Third-party advertising, analyticsYes*
Crash dataApp functionalityNo
Performance dataApp functionalityNo

* Tracking (personalized ads) only happens with your permission. On first launch iOS asks whether Magnet Pop may track you across apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are shown in a non-personalized way and the advertising identifier is not used to track you.

Third-party services

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google's handling of data is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy

How the data is used

Collected data is used to serve and measure advertisements (including, with your consent, personalized ads), to run basic analytics on ad performance, and to keep the app stable through crash and performance diagnostics. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and rights

  • Tracking: you can change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.
  • EU/EEA & UK (GDPR): you have the right to access, correct, or erase data and to object to processing. A consent form is presented where required.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you may request access to or deletion of personal information and opt out of its sale/sharing; we do not sell personal information.

Children

Magnet Pop is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where required, ads are served in a non-personalized manner for users below the applicable age of digital consent.

Data retention

On-device data (progress, settings) remains until you delete the app. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
